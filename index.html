How do we make this official?+

Send me a note with your date and the kind of story you're dreaming up. We'll arrange a relaxed call, and if it feels right, a signed agreement and retainer reserve your date.

What should we expect to invest?+

Wedding collections begin at ₹85,000, and portrait sessions at ₹15,000. Every collection is tailored to you; travel and applicable taxes may be additional.

Will you travel for our story?+

Absolutely. Jaipur is home, but I'm always happy to follow a good story. Travel is quoted transparently based on your location and plans.

When will we see our photographs?+

Your finished, hand-edited gallery arrives in a private online collection within 6–8 weeks for weddings and 3–4 weeks for portrait sessions.

Do we get to keep the photographs?+

Of course. Your collection includes personal printing rights. Photographs remain copyright of the photographer; commercial use is arranged separately.
